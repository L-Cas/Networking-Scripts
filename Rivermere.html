Welcome to Rivermere

Plant crops, sell your harvest or hold commodities until prices rise. Build your estate at your own pace. No enemies, no crop decay, no energy limit.

Farming

Choose a seed from the pouch, then click empty soil or use Plant empty plots. Seeds are paid for in gold. Ready crops glow and never spoil. Harvesting adds goods to the storehouse. If storage is full, crops stay planted. The seed choice does not affect harvesting.

Trading

Prices update every 30 seconds. Each commodity has its own volatility; town events alter demand. Buy prices are higher than sell prices, and both change over time. Use the ledger and market chart to make decisions. Buying and selling do not directly change the simulated market in this version.

Progression

Buy land, larger storage, better tools, improved soil, cheaper trading and seed discoveries. The first crops grow in 25–90 seconds; later crops take minutes. All prices and timings are prototype balancing values.

Saving & offline progress

The game saves to browser local storage after actions and periodically. Crop timers and market time continue while closed, using your device clock. Market catch-up is capped at 24 hours; crops can mature regardless. No internet, external scripts or account are needed. Export a save if local storage is blocked or before moving the HTML file. Import restores a save in this browser.
